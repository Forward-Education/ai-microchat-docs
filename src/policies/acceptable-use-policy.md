## Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the rules for using MicroChat. It applies to all users — students, educators, and administrators. By using the service, you agree to comply with this policy.

## Acceptable Use

You may use MicroChat to:

- Participate in educational activities and discussions
- Communicate with instructors, classmates, or team members in a respectful manner
- Submit work, share resources, and collaborate on assigned tasks
- Access tools and features provided within the platform for legitimate educational purposes

## Prohibited Conduct

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

### Harmful Content

- Posting, sharing, or transmitting content that is abusive, threatening, harassing, defamatory, obscene, or otherwise objectionable
- Sharing content that promotes discrimination based on race, gender, religion, nationality, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic

### Security Violations

- Attempting to gain unauthorized access to accounts, systems, or data
- Introducing malware, viruses, or malicious code
- Circumventing authentication or security controls
- Probing, scanning, or testing the vulnerability of the system

### Misuse of the Platform

- Using the service for commercial solicitation or spam
- Impersonating another user, instructor, or staff member
- Sharing login credentials or allowing others to access your account
- Automated scraping, harvesting, or data collection without authorization

### Academic Integrity

- Submitting others' work as your own
- Using the platform to facilitate cheating or academic dishonesty
- Sharing exam answers or assessment materials without authorization

## Reporting Violations

If you observe behavior that violates this policy, please report it to your instructor or platform administrator, or contact us directly at [support@microchat.co](mailto:support@microchat.co).

## Enforcement

Violations of this AUP may result in:

- A warning or temporary suspension of access
- Permanent termination of your account
- Reporting to your educational institution
- Legal action where required by law

The severity of the response will be proportionate to the nature and impact of the violation.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Acceptable Use Policy from time to time. Continued use of the service after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
