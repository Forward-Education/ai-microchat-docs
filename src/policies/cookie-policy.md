## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve efficiency, and provide information to site owners.

## How We Use Cookies

MicroChat uses cookies and similar technologies to:

- Keep you signed in to your account
- Remember your preferences and settings
- Understand how you use the service so we can improve it
- Ensure the security of your session

## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the service to function. They enable core features such as authentication and session management. You cannot opt out of these cookies while using the service.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Session token | Maintains your login session | Session |
| CSRF token | Protects against cross-site request forgery | Session |

### Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with MicroChat. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

### Preference Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make (such as language or display preferences) to provide a more personalized experience.

## Third-Party Cookies

Some features of MicroChat may use third-party services that set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies. Please refer to the respective privacy policies of those third parties for more information.

## Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the service. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions on managing cookies.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
