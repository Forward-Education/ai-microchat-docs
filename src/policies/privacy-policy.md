## Introduction

MicroChat and Forward Education ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and safeguard your information when you use our service at app.microchat.co.

MicroChat is built for K–12 educational settings. Where MicroChat is used by a school, school board, or other educational institution (a "School"), that institution is the entity responsible for the personal information of its students and staff. We act as a service provider (processor) on the School's behalf and under its direction. See [Roles and Responsibilities](#roles-and-responsibilities) below.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the service.

## Notice of Collection

This notice is provided in accordance with the *Municipal Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act* (MFIPPA) and, where applicable, the *Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act* (FIPPA).

- **What we collect:** Account identifiers (name, role, email or username), messages and content you submit, and technical/usage data (see [Information We Collect](#information-we-collect)).
- **Legal authority for collection:** Personal information is collected under the authority of the *Education Act* and the School's authority to operate educational programs, and is used by the School for educational purposes. MicroChat collects this information on the School's behalf.
- **Purpose of collection:** To provide and operate the MicroChat educational communication platform, to support teaching and learning, and to maintain the security and integrity of the service.
- **Questions about this collection** may be directed to your School's privacy office, or to MicroChat using the [contact details](#contact-us) at the end of this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

- Create or are provisioned an account
- Use chat or messaging features
- Contact us for support
- Respond to surveys or communications

This may include your name, email address or username, role (student, educator, administrator), class or group association, and any content you submit through the platform.

### Information Collected Automatically

When you use MicroChat, we may automatically collect:

- Device information (browser type, operating system)
- Log data (IP address, pages visited, time and date of visits)
- Usage data (features used, actions taken within the app)
- Cookies and similar technologies necessary to operate the service (see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/))

## Data Classification

We distinguish between categories of personal information so that student data receives the highest level of protection:

- **Student Personal Information:** Information that identifies, or could reasonably identify, a K–12 student — including name, username, class roster associations, and the content of messages. Student Personal Information is collected and processed solely on behalf of, and under the direction of, the School.
- **General User Information:** Information relating to educators, administrators, and other adult users acting in a professional capacity.

Where this policy grants protections specific to "students" or "Student Personal Information," those protections apply regardless of the user's age or jurisdiction.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **The School is the data controller / institution of record.** The School determines what information is collected, who may access it, and how long it is retained, and is responsible for obtaining any required consent.
- **MicroChat is the service provider (processor).** We process personal information only on the School's documented instructions and only to deliver, secure, and maintain the service. We do not use Student Personal Information for our own purposes.

## Consent

The consent model depends on how MicroChat is used:

- **Institutional consent:** When a School adopts MicroChat as part of its educational program, the School provides or manages consent on behalf of students under its institutional authority (for example, under the *Education Act* and the School's own privacy and consent practices).
- **Parental / guardian consent:** Where required by law or by School policy — including for students under the age of 13 — the School is responsible for obtaining verifiable parental or guardian consent before student accounts are created or used.

MicroChat relies on the School's confirmation that the appropriate consent has been obtained.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the MicroChat service
- Support teaching, learning, and communication within a School
- Communicate with users and Schools, including for support
- Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or misuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## Use of Student Data for Analytics and AI

- **No advertising or commercial profiling.** We do not use Student Personal Information to build advertising or behavioural profiles, and we do not use it to train third-party or general-purpose AI/ML models.
- **Service analytics use de-identified or aggregated data.** Any analytics we perform to operate and improve the service use aggregated or de-identified data that cannot reasonably be used to identify an individual student.
- **De-identification.** When we de-identify data, we remove or obscure direct and indirect identifiers and do not attempt to re-identify it.
- **No secondary use.** We do not use Student Personal Information for any purpose other than providing the service to the School. Any use beyond that requires the School's prior written authorization.

## No Sale or Profiling of Students

We **do not** and **will not**:

- Sell, rent, or trade Student Personal Information
- Use Student Personal Information for targeted advertising
- Create commercial or behavioural profiles of students

## Where Your Data Is Stored

MicroChat stores personal teacher/educator information on servers located in **Canada**.  Student data is not gathered or stored, and all chat data is anonymized via a PII inspector.

Data is processed outside of Canada, however that data is anonymized by our PII anonymizer service.

Where a School requires that all data remain within Canada, we will honour that requirement as set out in our agreement with the School.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as directed by the School.

- **Active accounts:** Retained for the duration of the user's enrolment or employment with the School.
- **Inactive accounts:** Deleted or anonymized within 12 months of an account becoming inactive, unless the School or local law directs otherwise.
- **Message and content data:** Retained for 5 years, unless a longer or shorter period is set by the School or local laws.
- **Backups and logs:** Purged on a rolling 365 day cycle.
- **Board-controlled retention:** A School may request export of its data, or request deletion at any time. On termination of service, we delete or anonymize School's data within 90 days or a date set by local laws.

### Secure Deletion

When data reaches the end of its retention period, it is securely deleted using methods designed to render it unrecoverable, including secure erasure of production data and removal from backups on the next backup cycle.

## Security

We implement technical and organizational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including:

- **Encryption in transit and at rest.** Data is encrypted in transit using TLS, and stored data is encrypted at rest.
- **Role-based access controls.** Access to personal information is restricted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis according to user and staff roles.
- **Audit logging.** Access to and changes affecting personal information are logged and monitored.
- **Breach notification.** If we become aware of a breach of security safeguards involving personal information, we will notify the affected School without undue delay and within **72 hours** of confirming the breach, and will support the School in meeting its own notification obligations.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect personal information using current industry practices.

## Your Rights

Students, guardians, and staff may have rights regarding their personal information — including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of it. Because the School is the institution of record, **requests are normally made through the School**, which may direct them to us. Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information held about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact your School's privacy office or contact us using the details below.

## Children's Privacy

MicroChat is designed for K–12 educational use, including students under the age of 13. We collect and process student information only on behalf of a School and only where the School has confirmed that appropriate institutional or parental/guardian consent is in place (see [Consent](#consent)). We comply with applicable laws governing the personal information of minors, including MFIPPA, FIPPA, PIPEDA, COPPA, and FERPA where applicable. If you believe we have collected information from a child without proper consent, please contact us immediately.
